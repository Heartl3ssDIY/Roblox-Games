Outline rendering repaired in Studio Edit mode, 2026-10-07.

Cause
377 Highlight objects existed across the DataModel: 160 in Workspace, 91 in ReplicatedStorage, 114 in ServerStorage archives/backups and 12 in StarterGui. Roblox supports 255 simultaneous Highlight instances; disabled instances also occupy slots. The new models' Highlight settings were present but their rendering slots were exhausted. The renderer also retained stale handles until those visible outlines were recreated.
Official reference: https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Highlight

Installed repair
149 exact stored outlines were converted into restorable metadata: 114 in explicitly named ServerStorage backup/archive folders and the 35 new ReplicatedStorage item templates.
All 56 old ReplicatedStorage Highlight objects were retained, including 51 old cosmetic-library/coin template outlines.
Full original property records and owner references are stored in ServerStorage.OutlinePropertyBackup20261007. Their restore round-trip passed before conversion. OriginalHighlightProperties.json is an additional local property backup; RestoreStoredOutlines.luau reconstructs the original objects if needed.
Every new expansion model uses true black outline metadata, with transparent fill and Occluded depth. The 15 currently visible new pet displays were refreshed and their black outlines were verified in an edit-time screenshot. Current new bats/toys remain in their requested storage locations.
PlaygroundPets now manages visible cosmetic outlines from model metadata. This covers all 86 cosmetic-library models (85 items plus the coin), including old templates which retain existing Highlights. Runtime clones receive outlines after entering Workspace; nearby, on-screen and locally equipped items are prioritized if the budget becomes tight. The manager caps cosmetic allocations and keeps 16 spare Highlight slots for transient game feedback.
No mesh shell was enlarged. Geometry, palettes, stud coatings, rigs and animation code were unchanged.
