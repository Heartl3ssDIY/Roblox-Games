# G-Blox Halloween 2026 planning notes

These notes capture the Halloween update decisions discussed on 3 October 2026. The event should clearly identify its content as Halloween 2026, with items that can only be obtained during that year's event. This document records plans; it does not change the game or its current event settings.

## Event dates

The confirmed event window is 17 October–1 November 2026, giving players roughly two weeks. This replaces the earlier suggestion of a 31 October launch.

The acquisition cutoff is 1 November 2026. Exact opening and closing times still need confirmation; the current unlimited-use cutoff is midnight at the start of 1 November, UK time. Additional content may arrive during the event week; the content and schedule are still undecided.

## Exclusive abilities

Pumpkin and Web should only be obtainable during the Halloween 2026 event. After the event closes, remove their purchase offers and reject new unlock purchases on the server. Merely hiding the shop cards is not sufficient.

Players who already own an ability keep it. They can still equip, use and recharge it after the event. Keep owned abilities visible in their inventory and provide a recharge route even when the new-purchase offers have disappeared.

The agreed ability settings remain:

- Pumpkin costs 150,000 cash to unlock. It has a 25-second cooldown and a 4-second orange splat.
- Web costs 350,000 cash to unlock. It has a 30-second cooldown and traps opponents for 5 seconds.
- Owners have unlimited use during the event without losing stored game uses.
- After unlimited use ends, cash recharges cost 15,000 for Pumpkin and 35,000 for Web. Each recharge gives one game use, matching the existing cash recharge system.
- Initial purchases preserve three game uses for after the event.

Label the abilities as Halloween 2026 exclusives. Do not automatically make the 2026 editions obtainable during Halloween 2027; later events may have different abilities or content.

## Ghostify ability

Ghostify is a planned new ability costing 500,000 cash. It can be used once per round within each game.

When its user dies, they become a player-controlled ghost morph that hunts the surviving racers. Players who die afterwards join the ghost team and also hunt the remaining players.

Ghosts stay active on the course and retain normal gravity-switch controls. They move faster than normal racers, but still have to avoid the same obstacles. They are not invincible: dying again fully eliminates them from the current round, and they must wait until the next round.

Reaching the finish as a ghost does not count as a win. Staying active as a ghost must not restore winner eligibility for that round.

The exact speed bonus, activation input and method for catching surviving racers have not been specified. Ghostify is recorded here only; it has not been implemented.

## Robux resets

Robux resets for Pumpkin, Web and Ghostify will be configured later. Prices and Roblox developer product IDs have not been supplied. Do not create or connect invented products in the meantime.

## Halloween egg and pets

A Halloween egg and Halloween pets are planned. Clearly distinguish their Halloween 2026 editions from future years. Models, pet names, prices, hatch chances and exact availability rules still need to be supplied or confirmed.

## Halloween title

Plan a title for people who participate in Halloween 2026 during the confirmed event window. The user will supply the title name. Exact eligibility and earning criteria still need confirmation. Make the year clear so participation in a future Halloween does not grant the 2026 title.

## Other holiday events

Use the same limited-time acquisition and retained-ownership approach for future holiday content, including Christmas, Easter and Chinese New Year. Define each event's year, dates and exclusives separately rather than automatically rereleasing earlier editions.
