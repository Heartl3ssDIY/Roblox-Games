PLAYGROUND CLASSICS / EVENT-BATS

HAND THIS WHOLE FOLDER TO THE BLENDER CHAT.
Create game-ready stylised models using the Originals PNGs as the visual reference. Each PNG is one distinct asset. Contact-Sheet.png is an overview, not a texture atlas. UI-512 and UI-256 are transparent shop icons of the same designs.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Chunky toy proportions, rounded bevels, broad glossy colour zones, deep navy seams and collars, cream inset panels and bold silhouettes. Build attached decorations as robust forms. Avoid tiny text, thin loose ornaments, photorealistic grunge or detached glow effects. Use the picture as the final visual reference if it differs slightly from the written concept.

MODELLING HANDOFF
These are single-view concept images, not measured turnarounds or existing 3D models. Infer unseen sides simply and consistently. Match the overall silhouette and visible colour blocking first. Keep materials reusable. Establish scale, pivot, triangle budget, collision and attachments in the Blender/game implementation chat before export. Bats need a handle grip pivot; other item pivots depend on their use. No gameplay or claim rules are implemented in these PNGs.

ROBLOX UI
Use the square UI exports with ScaleType Fit and a square UIAspectRatioConstraint, or retain the square aspect ratio yourself. Do not stretch them to fill a rectangular slot. Background transparency is already included. Do not bake item names, prices or counts into the artwork.

EVENT RULE
Birthday Wish is a player-birthday claim concept, NOT a game-anniversary reward. All six event bats are additional to the 15 core bats. Entitlement checking belongs in game logic.

ASSETS
E01 / Pumpkin Smacker / Halloween
Halloween baseball bat with a long segmented pumpkin-orange rounded barrel, a simple friendly jack-o-lantern face inset on its side, green collar with two small attached leaves, dark purple grip and orange knob. Keep a recognisable elongated bat, not a pumpkin on a stick.

E02 / Lucky Clover / St Patricks Day
Festive emerald-green baseball bat with an elongated rounded barrel, one large raised gold four-leaf-clover inlay, cream collar rings, deep green grip and gold knob. One small gold buckle decoration around the lower barrel. No hat, text or detached coins.

E03 / Festival Dragon / Lunar New Year
Lunar New Year-themed baseball bat with a long lacquer-red rounded barrel, broad raised gold cloud-scroll bands, a small rounded friendly dragon-head cap blended into the blunt tip, navy seams and cream grip with red end knob. Model-friendly chunky dragon muzzle, two short horns, no whisker filaments or loose ribbons, no text.

E04 / Peppermint Slugger / Christmas
Christmas baseball bat with a long rounded candy-cane red-and-ivory spiral barrel, a dark green chunky collar with two attached holly leaves and three red round berries, navy grip and red knob. Straight baseball-bat profile rather than a hooked candy cane, no writing.

E05 / Egg Hunt / Easter
Easter baseball bat with a long pastel-lavender rounded barrel, broad mint and pale-yellow egg-pattern bands, a small raised carrot-shaped orange inlay, cream collar, soft pink grip and mint knob. No detached eggs or rabbit characters, clear bat proportions.

E06 / Birthday Wish / Player birthday
A birthday cake-and-candles BASEBALL BAT: long cream-frosted rounded bat barrel with broad attached pink frosting drips and chunky cyan sprinkle inlays; at the blunt upper end three short thick pastel candles sit attached in a neat cluster, each with a tiny solid yellow teardrop flame. Lavender collar, pink wrapped grip and gold knob. The underlying silhouette must remain a full baseball bat, not a cake hammer. No text, logos, numbers or loose confetti.
Player-birthday claim concept only; NOT game-anniversary reward. Actual eligibility implementation belongs to game logic.
