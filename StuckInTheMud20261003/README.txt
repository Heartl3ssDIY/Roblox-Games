The default launch roster is two teams of two. MaxPlayers, MinimumPlayers, TeamCount and MaxTeamSize are editable arena attributes. The exact 2v2 defaults are MaxPlayers=4, MinimumPlayers=4, TeamCount=2 and MaxTeamSize=2; additional lobby players wait for another round. For two balanced teams containing up to twenty-four players use MaxPlayers=24, MinimumPlayers=2, TeamCount=2 and MaxTeamSize=12. For pairs across twenty-four players use MaxPlayers=24, MinimumPlayers=2, TeamCount=12 and MaxTeamSize=2. Team count automatically increases if needed to fit the selected roster at the configured maximum size, and may be reduced for fewer players. RoundDuration=300 seconds. SprintSeconds=10, SprintMultiplier=2.5 and SprintRefillSeconds=15 mean ten seconds of charge, 2.5 times walking speed and fifteen seconds to recharge fully. RescueSeconds=5 requires continuous heat from a teammate. ProjectileSpeed=115, MudRange=130, MudCooldownSeconds=0.65 and HeatRange=24 control the guns. Tag receives the same sprint charge, multiplier and refill defaults. Existing arena settings are preserved on repeat installs.

A mud team is eliminated when everyone on it is stuck. At timeout, the highest fraction of free players divided by that team's original size wins; tied teams share the win. Normal completed rounds give winners 150 coins and one win, and give other completed participants 50 coins. Every completed participant receives 50 XP. Practice rounds and canceled rounds pay nothing. Lobby attributes WinnerCoins=150, LoserCoins=50, WinnerXP=50, LoserXP=50, BaseLevelXP=200 and LevelXPStep=50 are applied by this upgrade. Levels start at 1; the next threshold is 200 + 50*(level-1). Stored lifetime XP remains integer data and is not reset by installation.
