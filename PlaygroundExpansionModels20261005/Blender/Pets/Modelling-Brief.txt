PLAYGROUND CLASSICS / PETS

HAND THIS WHOLE FOLDER TO THE BLENDER CHAT.
Create game-ready stylised models using the Originals PNGs as the visual reference. Each PNG is one distinct asset. Contact-Sheet.png is an overview, not a texture atlas. UI-512 and UI-256 are transparent shop icons of the same designs.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Chunky toy proportions, rounded bevels, broad glossy colour zones, deep navy seams and collars, cream inset panels and bold silhouettes. Build attached decorations as robust forms. Avoid tiny text, thin loose ornaments, photorealistic grunge or detached glow effects. Use the picture as the final visual reference if it differs slightly from the written concept.

MODELLING HANDOFF
These are single-view concept images, not measured turnarounds or existing 3D models. Infer unseen sides simply and consistently. Match the overall silhouette and visible colour blocking first. Keep materials reusable. Establish scale, pivot, triangle budget, collision and attachments in the Blender/game implementation chat before export. Bats need a handle grip pivot; other item pivots depend on their use. No gameplay or claim rules are implemented in these PNGs.

ROBLOX UI
Use the square UI exports with ScaleType Fit and a square UIAspectRatioConstraint, or retain the square aspect ratio yourself. Do not stretch them to fill a rectangular slot. Background transparency is already included. Do not bake item names, prices or counts into the artwork.

INDEX SILHOUETTES
Silhouettes-Originals, Silhouettes-512 and Silhouettes-256 use the same outline and pose as the corresponding colour image, with solid dark interiors and antialiased boundary pixels. Match files by their ID/filename. Do not re-model a silhouette as a separate pet. The dark fill is RGB 21,33,51.
