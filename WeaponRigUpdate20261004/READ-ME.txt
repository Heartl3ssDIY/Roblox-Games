Installed into the open Playground Classics place in Roblox Studio, in Edit mode.

Behaviour
- All 15 currently available catalogue bats are native, undroppable Tools.
- Bats equip only for Bulldogs during Crossing and the current Tagger during Playing.
- Click/tap requests a swing. The server validates the active game, role, character, cooldown, frontal arc, range, line of sight and target protection.
- Cooldown: 0.75 seconds. Windup: 0.16 seconds. Impact window ends at 0.30 seconds. One target per swing.
- Successful hits preserve each game's scoring/role rules and apply a 1.35-second ragdoll.
- Existing individual held VFX, impact bursts, cartoon outlines and stud layers are retained.
- Hand grips and procedural upper-body swing poses support native R6, legacy Motor6D R15, and current AnimationConstraint R15.
- The former Tagger retains a disabled bat briefly to finish the visible followthrough; it cannot hit again.
- MudGun and HeatGun remain exclusive to active Stuck in the Mud participants. Round/owner checks reject stale or foreign tools. Both guns have matching palm attachments, correct forward muzzle orientation, and noncolliding/massless art.
- Admin item grants/equip selection, coins and levels remain available. Lobby bat preview was removed; bat selection is used in the appropriate game.
