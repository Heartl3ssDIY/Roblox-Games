# Arena character shortlist — draft for review

35 character variants across seven non–Dragon Ball arena pools. Dragon Ball choices are reserved for you. These are **provisional strength-based shortlists, not canonical ordered top-five rankings**: several arcs do not establish enough head-to-head evidence for an exact top five. The cutoff notes and alternate pools avoid silently treating later forms as early units.

Proposed ordinary rarity ladder: **Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary**. Rarity is a game-balance suggestion informed by strength within the pool, not a literal cross-universe power scale. Mystical is excluded from all these normal arena pools. Most of a strongest-character shortlist should be Epic/Legendary; a complete pack needs additional Common/Rare characters.

## Arena 2: Chūnin Exam Hall — Naruto

**Cutoff:** Part I: Chūnin Exams / immediate Konoha invasion period.

Strong adult fighters associated with this period. These are not the five strongest examinees. Ordering within the top group is debatable.

- **Orochimaru — Legendary.** Part I; snakes, sword and forbidden techniques. No later-body upgrades.
- **Jiraiya — Legendary.** Part I; summons and standard ninjutsu. Do not import Shippuden Sage Mode feats.
- **Hiruzen Sarutobi — Legendary.** Elder Third Hokage version, not hypothetical prime Hiruzen.
- **Might Guy — Epic.** Part I taijutsu specialist; do not give him the war-arc Eighth Gate kit.
- **Kakashi Hatake — Epic.** Part I Sharingan / Lightning Blade; no Kamui or double Mangekyō.

**Alternative:** If you mean exam contestants only: Gaara (Epic), Naruto (Epic, finals version), Sasuke (Epic, Chidori), Neji (Rare), Rock Lee (Rare, limited Gates). Lee/Neji and the top three are matchup-dependent.

[Arc/character reference](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1282) supports the setting and characters; the proposed rarity and comparative selection are design judgments.

## Arena 3: Loguetown — One Piece

**Cutoff:** East Blue / Loguetown, before entering the Grand Line.

Present-day visitors and fighters; exclude Roger's historical execution. Dragon is a provisional inclusion: this arc does not reveal enough of his combat ability to rank him confidently.

- **Monkey D. Dragon — Legendary.** Provisional mentor/boss candidate. No invented confirmed Devil Fruit or wind move set.
- **Smoker — Legendary.** Loguetown captain with smoke powers and seastone jitte; no later vice-admiral upgrades.
- **Monkey D. Luffy — Epic.** East Blue rubber-fighting version; no Gears or Haki.
- **Roronoa Zoro — Rare.** East Blue three-sword style; no Asura, Enma or Haki.
- **Sanji — Rare.** East Blue kicking style; no Diable Jambe, raid suit or later enhancements.

**Alternative:** If only demonstrated local combat counts, leave Dragon unranked and use Buggy (Rare) as the fifth playable pack candidate.

[Arc/character reference](https://one-piece.com/character/smoker/index.html) supports the setting and characters; the proposed rarity and comparative selection are design judgments.

## Arena 4: Jujutsu High — Jujutsu Kaisen

**Cutoff:** Season 1 Kyoto Goodwill Event / school attack.

A strong shortlist from the event and its attacking/supporting cast, not a settled canonical top five. The training pairing is Yuji and Todo; Yuta belongs to a different roster decision.

- **Satoru Gojo — Legendary.** Adult Season 1 version; adapt Infinity to counterable game mechanics.
- **Hanami — Legendary.** Goodwill Event version; use the abilities demonstrated in this encounter.
- **Mahito — Legendary.** Season 1 version; no later Shibuya final transformation.
- **Aoi Todo — Epic.** Goodwill Event Boogie Woogie; no later prosthetic/vibraslap version.
- **Mei Mei — Epic.** Grade 1 supporting sorcerer. Exact ranking and a strict Season 1-only combat kit remain uncertain.

**Alternative:** For the student training theme instead: Todo (Epic), Yuji (Rare/Epic), Megumi (Rare), Toge (Rare), Maki (Rare, pre-awakening). Yuta should be a separately labelled JJK 0 version if you choose him.

[Arc/character reference](https://jujutsukaisen.jp/character/index_1st.php) supports the setting and characters; the proposed rarity and comparative selection are design judgments.

## Arena 5: Mount Fujikasane — Demon Slayer

**Cutoff:** Opening training and Final Selection period, through episode 5.

Early-arc shortlist including mentors and the selection boss. Giyu and Urokodaki are not Final Selection contestants. Early Kanao/Urokodaki relative strength is inferred, not settled by direct fights in these episodes.

- **Giyu Tomioka — Legendary.** Opening-series Hashira; no later mark or final-battle upgrades.
- **Sakonji Urokodaki — Epic.** Retired mentor version; not an invented prime form.
- **Kanao Tsuyuri — Epic.** Final Selection candidate; no later eye technique or endgame feats.
- **Tanjiro Kamado — Rare.** Final Selection Water Breathing; blue cloud haori. No Hinokami Kagura or mark.
- **Hand Demon — Rare.** Final Selection boss; grab/arm pressure, with a readable counter.

**Alternative:** If you want only the five surviving recruits: Kanao (Epic), Tanjiro (Rare), Zenitsu (Rare), Inosuke (Rare), Genya (Common). Their exact strength order cannot be established from Final Selection alone.

[Arc/character reference](https://demonslayer-anime.com/risshihen/character/) supports the setting and characters; the proposed rarity and comparative selection are design judgments.

## Arena 6: Heavens Arena — Hunter × Hunter

**Cutoff:** 2011 anime Heavens Arena arc; before Yorknew.

Five practical arc-associated combat candidates. Wing's full fighting strength is not shown, and Machi's brief appearance does not establish a reliable comparison; this is not an official ranking.

- **Hisoka — Legendary.** Heavens Arena Bungee Gum and trickery; no later manga developments.
- **Wing — Epic.** Nen teacher. Full combat kit is unspecified; keep any game adaptation labelled as such.
- **Kastro — Epic.** Tiger Bite Fist and Double, as demonstrated against Hisoka.
- **Killua Zoldyck — Rare.** Early assassin training and basic Nen; no electricity or Godspeed.
- **Gon Freecss — Rare.** Heavens Arena basic Nen and physical fighting; no Jajanken or adult form.

**Alternative:** Zushi is a natural Common addition for the wider pack pool. A strict strongest-five list could include Machi, but her placement is uncertain.

[Arc/character reference](https://www.ntv.co.jp/hunterhunter/story/032.html) supports the setting and characters; the proposed rarity and comparative selection are design judgments.

## Arena 8: Wano — One Piece

**Cutoff:** Onigashima raid resolution; exclude historical flashbacks and post-raid arrivals.

Leading raid combatants. Strength ordering among Law, Kid, Yamato and other contenders is debatable. Using the Flower Capital arrival date instead would remove these later raid upgrades.

- **Kaido — Legendary.** Wano raid version; keep base and hybrid forms explicitly labelled.
- **Monkey D. Luffy — Wano — Legendary.** Gear 5 only if the arena pool covers the end of the raid. Otherwise use Gear 4.
- **Charlotte Linlin / Big Mom — Legendary.** Wano version with her homies; no invented post-Wano form.
- **Trafalgar D. Water Law — Epic.** Raid version; awakened abilities only under the end-of-raid cutoff.
- **Eustass Kid — Epic.** Raid version; awakened magnetism only under the end-of-raid cutoff.

**Alternative:** Yamato is a credible substitution for Law or Kid. If the intended date is the Flower Capital arrival, use pre-raid versions and revisit the shortlist.

[Arc/character reference](https://one-piece.com/story/land_of_wano2/index.html) supports the setting and characters; the proposed rarity and comparative selection are design judgments.

## Arena 9: Marineford — One Piece

**Cutoff:** Opening frozen-bay battle, before Shanks's arrival.

Five frontline powerhouses for the iced-over bay, not an uncontested verse ranking. Garp and Sengoku are strong alternatives; their exact order relative to these five is unsettled.

- **Edward Newgate / Whitebeard — Legendary.** Old, ill Marineford version; not prime Whitebeard.
- **Dracule Mihawk — Legendary.** Marineford swordsman; no later bounty-based scaling.
- **Sakazuki / Akainu — Legendary.** Pre-timeskip admiral; no post-timeskip fleet-admiral assumptions.
- **Kuzan / Aokiji — Legendary.** Pre-timeskip admiral and the bay's ice theme; not post-Punk Hazard appearance.
- **Borsalino / Kizaru — Legendary.** Pre-timeskip admiral; no later Egghead feats.

**Alternative:** If the whole war is included, Shanks becomes eligible. Garp and Sengoku also belong on the wider strongest-candidate list.

[Arc/character reference](https://one-piece.com/story/marineford/index.html) supports the setting and characters; the proposed rarity and comparative selection are design judgments.
