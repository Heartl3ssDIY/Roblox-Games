This integration connects the existing imports in Studio: 15 core bats, 20 pets,
15 miscellaneous toys, the collectible coin, and the Mud/Heat guns.
The original imported models remain in their workspace locations. Their backups
are in ServerStorage.AssetIntegrationBackup20261004.ImportedModels.

The live editable catalogue is ReplicatedStorage.PlaygroundCatalog. The complete
reward/price list is Unlock-Schedule.csv. Starter Slugger is free. Mudslinger is
earned at 25 Stuck in the Mud wins. Coin prices never exceed 10,000. Level and win
rewards are granted automatically; coins cannot bypass their requirements.
Existing balances, XP, ownership and total wins are retained. Per-game win counts
start from this update because old profiles stored only total wins.

Players choose a bat skin in the shop; it appears when they become a Bulldog.
Bat skins do not change tag distance or damage. Toys are permanent collectible
purchases and equip from the Backpack between rounds. Their gameplay actions
and potion effects remain undecided, as requested previously.

The shop reuses its 12 editable cards in StarterGui.PlaygroundShell, with All,
Bats, Pets and Toys filters and five pages. Labels, outlines and pager are editable.
Runtime binding does not replace authored UI positions or sizes.

Pet motion runs locally for smooth following, bounce/hover and restrained sparkles.
All imported controllers, Motor6Ds and pose data are preserved. Pocket Bulldog
has two Motor6Ds; the other imports currently have AnimationControllers without
joint/bone animation clips. Whole-model motion works with those imports and the
follower supports future Idle/Walk Animation children.

Textured imports keep their original colour maps, with a welded, slightly expanded
stud surface layer. It is named PCStudFinish and can be edited or removed per mesh.
Plain gun meshes use PlaygroundItemStuds directly. CartoonOutline is editable on
each model. Mud is brown/green; Heat is orange/cream. Playable gun templates now
use the imported artwork and preserve the existing fire/equip interfaces.

2,452 lobby parts are anchored and locked. Imported display models are also
anchored and locked, while equipped clones are non-colliding and massless.
524 targeted surface edits remove detected coplanar colour conflicts in Tag and
Mud; walkable floor heights and all saved collision flags remain unchanged.

These map/stand property backups are in ServerStorage.AssetIntegrationBackups.
