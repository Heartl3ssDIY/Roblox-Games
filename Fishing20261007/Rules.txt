Fishing: four fixed rod stations around the lobby pond.
Each player can complete three catches per UTC day. The count persists across rejoining and servers.
Each catch waits six seconds at the rod, then randomly grants either Coins or XP with an equal 50% chance.
Walking away, dying, or entering a round before the catch cancels without spending a daily catch.
Each successful catch activates immediately, adding five minutes to its matching timer; there is no potion inventory.
Coins and XP have separate timers. Repeated catches of the same type extend its remaining duration.
Boosts multiply the player's current earned rate. VIP becomes 6x coins or 4x XP while the corresponding fishing boost is active.
Purchased coin packs and admin grants stay at their exact amount.
Timers continue in real time and persist across rejoining. Icons/countdowns live in the bottom-right HUD and disappear on expiry.
